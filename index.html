Listening recording

Press play and answer as you listen. This recording stays at the top of the screen while you scroll. Each part is played twice in the recording.

Instructions: Answer all 30 questions. For Parts 1 to 3, tap one answer for each question. For Part 4, type no more than one word in each blank space. When you finish, scroll to the bottom to submit and see your score.

Part 1

Questions 1 to 7

You will hear people talking in seven different situations. For questions 1 to 7, choose the correct answer (A, B or C).

Part 2

Questions 8 to 15

You will hear a student, Grace, talking about a work experience at a community fair. For questions 8 to 15, choose the correct answer (A, B or C).

Part 3

Questions 16 to 20

You will hear five short extracts in which people are talking about their unique jobs. For questions 16 to 20, choose from the list (A to G) what each speaker says. Use each letter only once. There are two extra letters which you do not need to use.

    Part 4

    Questions 21 to 30

    You will hear two friends, Iman and Shasha, talking about shopping. For questions 21 to 30, fill in the missing information in each numbered space. Use NO MORE THAN ONE WORD for each space.
